34. Very important distinction: WhatsApp connection

There are two fundamentally different approaches.

Option A — Official WhatsApp Business Platform

Your App

 ↓

Your Backend

 ↓

Meta WhatsApp Business Platform

 ↓

WhatsApp

Good for:

business messaging

APIs

webhooks

sending/receiving supported messages

But it is not equivalent to cloning a personal WhatsApp account / WhatsApp Web experience.

Option B — WhatsApp Web/session automation

Conceptually:

Your App

 ↓

Backend

 ↓

WhatsApp Web session

 ↓

WhatsApp

This can resemble the experience you're describing much more closely, but it introduces major reliability, account-policy, security and maintenance concerns and should not be treated as a production-safe equivalent to an official API.

So before coding the QR/login layer, we should decide which WhatsApp integration is actually permitted and technically suitable for your target use case.
