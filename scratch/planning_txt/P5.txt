62. WhatsApp integration warning

There is one architectural issue you must settle before implementation.

Your original requirement is essentially:

Connect a user's personal WhatsApp like WhatsApp Web, show their chats, receive/send real-time messages, and mirror them into your application.

The official WhatsApp Business Platform is not simply a drop-in personal WhatsApp Web clone. If you use an unofficial WhatsApp Web/session automation approach, you need to account for provider/platform terms, session reliability, QR authentication, disconnections and potential account restrictions.

So don't build your entire production architecture assuming:

QR → personal WhatsApp → unlimited WhatsApp Web clone

until the exact integration mechanism is selected.

The rest of the architecture above is deliberately designed so the WhatsApp integration is an adapter:

       │

       ├── connect()

       ├── disconnect()

       ├── sync()

       ├── sendMessage()

       ├── receiveMessage()

       └── messageStatus()

That way, if you change the provider later, your:

don't need to be rewritten.

And the single most important design rule for your product is:

Every piece of data—messages, contacts, chats, media, AI memories, embeddings, caches and WebSocket events—must always have an unambiguous path back to the authenticated user and their connected WhatsApp account.

If that rule is enforced consistently, two users can have contacts with the same names/numbers without their data ever being mixed.
